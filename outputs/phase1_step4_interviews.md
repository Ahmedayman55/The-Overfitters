# Phase 1, step 4: Customer interviews (Mom Test) for 'A practice workbook of SQL interview questions with worked answers for junior data analysts preparing for their first job'

## Method
- Talk about their life and past behaviour, not about our idea.
- Ask about specifics in the past, not opinions about the future ('would you buy...?').
- Talk less, listen more. No pitching during the interview.
- Compliments and 'sounds great' are not evidence. Commitments (time, money, intro) are.

## Interview guide
1. Tell me about the last time you dealt with junior and aspiring data analysts find sql interview questions hard to get right on their own.
2. What was the hardest part? Why?
3. What did you try to solve it? What did that cost you in time or money?
4. What don't you love about the things you tried?
5. How are you handling it right now?
6. Is there anyone else I should talk to about this?

What counts as a strong signal:
- They already spent money or hours on a workaround.
- They ask when they can get it, unprompted.
- They offer an intro to someone else with the problem.
